Common questions

Does my partner need the app too?

Yes. Locket is made for two. Once you’re set up, share your invite code and they join your locket from their own iPhone.

Is Locket only for iPhone?

For now, yes. Locket is on iPhone today.

Who can see what we add?

Only the two of you. There are no public profiles and no ads, and private notes are visible only to the person who wrote them.

Do we both need Premium?

No. When one of you subscribes, Premium covers both of you.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Locket. You can cancel any time.

I got a new phone. How do I get Premium back?

Sign in to Locket, open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my account. Your private notes, drawings and profile are deleted for good. Memories you made together stay with your partner if they’re still using Locket, and are deleted too once neither of you is. You can also email us for help.

My partner’s invite link opens a web page instead of the app

Make sure Locket is installed on their iPhone first, then tap the invite link again. On the web page, tap “Open in Locket”. If it still doesn’t work, write to us and we’ll help you connect.

I didn’t get my confirmation or password email

Check your spam or promotions folder, and make sure you typed the address you signed up with. Still nothing after a few minutes? Email us from that address and we’ll sort it out.
